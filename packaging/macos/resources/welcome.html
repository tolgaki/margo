Margo — an AI chief of staff for Microsoft 365

This installs the Margo agent and her skills into your GitHub Copilot CLI directory, so you can ask for things like:

  • “Brief me.”
  • “Triage my inbox.”
  • “Find 30 minutes with Dana this week.”
  • “Draft a reply to that.”

Margo reads your mail, calendar, Teams and files through WorkIQ, and proposes — she never sends, replies, RSVPs or deletes anything without your explicit approval.

Where things go. Files are staged in /usr/local/share/margo and installed into ~/.copilot for your account only.

Upgrading is safe. Your preferences.md, commitments.md, config.md and saved state are never overwritten.
