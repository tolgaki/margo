Margo - an AI chief of staff for Microsoft 365

This installs the Margo agent and her skills into your GitHub Copilot CLI
directory, so you can ask for things like:

    "Brief me."
    "Triage my inbox."
    "Find 30 minutes with Dana this week."
    "Draft a reply to that."

Margo reads your mail, calendar, Teams and files through Work IQ, and PROPOSES.
She never sends, replies, RSVPs or deletes anything without your explicit
approval of that specific action.


WHERE THINGS GO

  Program files    %LOCALAPPDATA%\Margo
  Agent and skills %USERPROFILE%\.copilot

This is a per-user install, so it does not need administrator rights.


UPGRADING IS SAFE

Your personal files are never overwritten or deleted:

  preferences.md    who you are, who matters, how you write
  commitments.md    what you owe, what you're waiting on
  config.md         per-skill settings
  state\            saved state for scheduled runs

Uninstalling backs them up rather than removing them.


REQUIREMENTS

  GitHub Copilot CLI
  A Microsoft 365 work account, reachable through Work IQ
  Python 3.9 or later, for the bundled helper scripts
